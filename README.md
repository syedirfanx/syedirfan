# Syed Irfan's Personal Website & Portfolio

A modern, responsive static portfolio and personal website showcasing academic research, professional experience, project portfolios, and visual life stories.

## Highlights

- **Home & Biography**: Overview of projects, skills, and research background.
- **Academic & Research**: Academic achievements, research focus areas, and publications.
- **Experience**: Detailed career timeline and key achievements.
- **The Person**: Interests and personal reflections.
- **The Odyssey**: An interactive photo gallery and travelogue organized into curated photo albums.
- **Blog**: Personal essays, tech insights, and thoughts.
- **Contact**: Direct contact form and social links.

---

## Tech Stack

- **HTML5 & Vanilla JavaScript**
- **Tailwind CSS (CDN)**
- **Lucide Icons**
- **Vite**
